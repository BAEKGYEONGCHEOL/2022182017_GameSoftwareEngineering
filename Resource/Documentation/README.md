# Dark Sci-Fi Modular Blender Asset Pack

- Blender 5.2 authoring source, metric units, +Z up, -Y forward.
- Low-poly modular humanoid uses a 7-head heroic proportion and HUM_v1 shared skeleton.
- The pulse rifle is a multi-part runtime asset with receiver, barrel, muzzle, stock, grip, magazine, optic, rail, energy coils and status lighting.
- Rifle animation clips include equip-to-aim, breathing aim idle and firing recoil poses for upper-body layering after weapon pickup.
- Humanoid and void rigs include distinct fall and collapse death actions.
- Void quadruped is an original low-center, four-legged assault creature with VOID_v1 modular overlays.
- Ship controls, pilot seat, medical bed and power junction include rounded housings, articulated supports, hydraulics, conduits and emissive instruments.
- Ship walls and floor tiles use a 2 m module and 0.25 m snap grid.
- Materials use dark gunmetal, oxidized armor, cold cyan displays, warning amber, desaturated skin and violet-red alien tissue.
- Animation clips include walk, run, left/right turn, attack, jump, rifle aiming, death, turn interpolation poses, and walk/run transitions.
- Projectile meshes use a centered pivot and -Y forward axis; gameplay controls their translation.

## Libraries

- `DarkSciFi_ModularAssetPack.blend`: complete source and showcase scene.
- `Libraries/Humanoid_Modular.blend`: human rig, body parts, clothing, equipment, rifle and actions.
- `Libraries/Void_Quadruped.blend`: quadruped rig, modular parts, actions.
- `Libraries/Ship_ModularKit.blend`: walls, controls, medical devices, floor tiles/materials.
- `Libraries/Space_Asteroids.blend`: four low-poly asteroids and drift-spin actions.
- `Libraries/Projectile_Meshes.blend`: ballistic, armor-piercing, plasma projectiles and spent casing.

See `AnimationAndAssembly.json` for clip mapping and blend recommendations.
